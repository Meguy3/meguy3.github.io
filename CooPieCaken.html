Chocolate Cherry CooPieCaken

Step 1: Pie

Ingredients:

  • 2 unbaked pie crust

  • 2 cans cherry pie filling

  • 1 teaspoons almond extract

  • Milk

  • Sugar

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 425.

  2. Place one pie crust in a deep dish pie plate. Push it around firmly into the dish.

  3. Open the cans of pie filling and scrape it into the pie plate.

  4. Add the almond extract and mix it with the cherries and juice. Smooth it all out with a spatula.

  5. Lay the remaining pie crust on a large cutting board.

  6. Cut out four pieces of crust to vent from.

  7. Carefully lay the second pie crust on top of the pie filling, and press the edges together.

  8. Brush the top crust with milk and sprinkle with sugar.

  9. Bake until the crust is golden brown, about 40-45 minutes. After the first 15 minutes, cover the outer edge of the pie with aluminum foil or a pie shield to make sure it doesn't get too brown.

Step 2: Cookies & Cake

Part 1: Cake

  • Ingredients:

      • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

      • 1 cup sugar

      • 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa

      • 1/2 teaspoon salt

      • 1 teaspoon baking soda

      • 12 Bing cherries, pitted and cut in half

      • 2-3 teaspoons pure vanilla extract

      • 1 serving cup cherry Greek-style yogurt

      • 2 teaspoons apple cider, red or white wine vinegar

      • 2 tablespoons cherry juice

      • Coffee to fill to the one cup line (Water works as well)

      • 1/3 cup vegetable oil

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.  Grease and flour a 9-inch cake pan; set aside.

  2. Put the flour, sugar, cocoa, salt, baking soda, and ground coffee beans in a largish bowl; stir with a whisk.  Add the halved cherries; toss with the dry ingredients.  Make a well in the center.

  3. Place the yogurt, cherry juice, vanilla extract in a cup measure.  Fill to the one cup level with cold coffee.  Stir well to combine; pour into the dry ingredients; add the oil.  Using a spatula, stir well until the batter is smooth. Pour into the prepared pan; smooth the top (this is a thick batter). 

Part 2: Cookies

Ingredients:

  • Brownies:

  • 10 tablespoon unsalted butter

  • 1 ¼ cups white sugar

  • ¾ cup cocoa powder

  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt

  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

  • 2 large eggs

  • ½ cup flour

  • Oreos:

  • 1 package of Oreos (Family size, double stuff is better)

  • Cookies:

  • ½ cup unsalted butter at room temp

  • ¼ cup brown sugar

  • ¾ cup white sugar

  • 1 egg

  • 1 ¼ teaspoons vanilla extract

  • 1 ¼ cups flour

  • ½ teaspoon salt

  • ½ teaspoon baking soda

  • ½ teaspoon baking powder

  • 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions:

  1. Brownies:

  2. In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium high heat.

  3. Add the sugar and cocoa powder once the butter is melted.

  4. Whisk to combine and remove from heat.

  5. Add the salt, vanilla and eggs and continuously whisk until the eggs are combined.

  6. Add the flour and continue to mix. Set batter aside.

  7. Cookies:

  8. Cream together the butter and sugars in a mixer.

  9. Add the egg and vanilla, making sure to scrape down the sides of the mixing bowl.

  10. Add the flour, salt, baking soda and baking powder and mix on low until everything is incorporated. Fold in the chocolate chips. Set dough aside.

  11. Oreos:

  12. Line the bottom of a 9 inch skillet with tin foil or parchment paper and then spray the foil/paper with a spritz of non-stick baking spray.

  13. Layer the cookie dough on the bottom of a 9x9 baking pan, pressing down to form the bottom of the cookies.

  14. Layer as many Oreos that will fit on top of the cookie dough. No need to overlap. One single layer will do.

  15. Pour the brownie batter on top of the Oreos layer and make sure it's evenly layers on top.

Part 3: Bake

Bake both at 350 for 30-50 minutes or until no longer sticky with a knife.

Step 3: Icing;

Ingredients:

  • 2 cup butter (softened)

  • 8 cups powdered sugar

  • 4 ounces unsweetened chocolate (melted)

  • 6-8 tablespoons cherry juice

Instructions:

  1. In a mixer bowl, beat the butter for 2-3 minutes until it is creamy and almost white in appearance.

  2. Beat in the powdered sugar, melted chocolate and 2 tablespoons of cherry juice until light and fluffy. (Add in additional juice, one tablespoon at a time, until the frosting is the consistency you like.)

Final Step: Building

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup cherries

Instructions:

  1. Use a knife to remove the cookie/brownies part from the skillet. Flip over onto a plate so that it rests on the brownies. Flip it over onto the serving plate so that brownies are on top.

  2. Place frosting over the brownies

  3. Cut the pie from the pie plate with a butter knife and flip onto a flat plate.

  4. Flip from flat plate to onto top of the brownies.

  5. If done successfully and pie is whole, coat with frosting. Otherwise, coat with even more frosting.

  6. Flip cake onto pie very gently and make sure not to break it.

  7. Coat entire thing in icing until you run out of icing.

  8. Top with extra cherries, and serve immediately.
